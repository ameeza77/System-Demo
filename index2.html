Who We Are

A Home Kitchen with a Big Heart

HomeBakerz started in 2008 from a passion for baking and a desire to share homemade goodness with friends and family. Over 16 years later, we've grown into a trusted home-based bakery with hundreds of happy customers.

We specialise in artisan cookies, celebration cakes, and pastries using quality ingredients — no preservatives, no shortcuts. Every order is personalised and baked fresh upon order.
